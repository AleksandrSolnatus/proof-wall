Corrections are contributions

Leave a useful trail.

A counterexample, a clearer method, a missing source, or an unexpected connection can all move the work forward.

Start with one exhibit

  1. Name its stable ID and version.
  2. Describe your correction, question, or connection. Separate what you observed from what you infer.
  3. Include reproducible evidence and its limits. Share only material you have permission to share.

Download the contribution template · Exhibit JSON schema · Curator guide (local toolkit required)

Send it for review

Contact Bridges at hello.bridges.group@gmail.com. Include the exhibit reference. Ask for a private handoff before sending sensitive material.

No message is sent by this page. There is no public upload form. Proposed contributions remain outside the research record until reviewed.

For the curator

Prepare one directory containing exhibit.json, article.md, and explicitly listed evidence files. Validate it locally, then hand the unchanged package to the owner for publication review. The builder accepts only the exact approved snapshot.
