AREPO 012 | INVARIANT LADDER (QUICK REFERENCE)

1  CONGRUENCE:       Equal distances — rotation / translation / reflection.
2  SIMILARITY:      Equal proportions — positive uniform scale allowed.
3  SIMPLE LOOP:     Continuous, one-to-one closed boundary — may bend.
4  RELATIONSHIP:    Labeled fragments and their source adjacency survive a cut.
5  ORIGIN RECORD:   Source and transformations can be traced externally.
6  LOSS BOUNDARY:   Two originals produce identical retained evidence; unique
                   source recognition is then impossible from that evidence.

WARNING: these are DIFFERENT criteria, not one strict total ordering; topology
and source history can be independent. A ledger may survive total geometric
collapse, while a visible shape may survive without any source history.

Circle R -> R/2 : C -> C/2, A -> A/4, pi stays pi.
Radial test r(theta)=R[1+a cos(3theta)]:
  0<a<1 simple loop; a=1 pinches; a>1 crosses.
Split: original boundary becomes two labeled arcs.
Collapse to point: destroys coordinate injectivity.

Play, test, and name what remains — don't certify cosmic conclusions.
