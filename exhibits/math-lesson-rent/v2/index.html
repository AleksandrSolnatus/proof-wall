Reuse pays rent; changed weights invalidate an answer index

verified finite result

Bounded historical result described by the retained source and receipts; no fresh scientific execution in this gathering.

The question

When does a reusable answer index repay its build cost, and what happens when its input changes?

The method

Compare retained indexed and structural searches across tested batch sizes, then reuse a stale index after a weight change and test a guarded rebuild.

The finding

Cold index loses to structural search at one query but wins at tested sizes 4,16,64. Changing one weight makes old index wrong on 23/64 queries; explicit identity comparison and rebuilding restore correctness.
