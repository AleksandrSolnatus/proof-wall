# Reuse pays rent; changed weights invalidate an answer index

Cold index loses to structural search at one query but wins at tested sizes 4,16,64. Changing one weight makes old index wrong on 23/64 queries; explicit identity comparison and rebuilding restore correctness.

## What was checked

Independent source review and numerical checking, complete witness comparisons and retained stale negative controls.

The attached records are existing evidence, copied intact or extracted exactly as disclosed in source-selections.json. No experiment was rerun for this exhibit.

## Limits

- One 16-position toy family; crossover only bracketed between 1 and 4.
- Mutation detection/rebuild is an explicit scenario, not general cache service.
- No total model-cost or general runtime benefit claim.
