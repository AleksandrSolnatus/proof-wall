## Mechanical dictionary
Eight-connected ink and four-connected white; enclosed white regions are holes. Skeleton uses the two-pass Zhang–Suen thinning rules implemented in code/geometry.py, capped at 2000 iterations. Remove endpoint-to-junction spurs of Euclidean graph length <=.03R, iteratively until unchanged, maximum20 rounds. Retain connected outlines; no exclusion by P-33 component count.

E: skeleton pixels of eight-neighbor degree1, clustered by8-connectivity. J: pixels degree>=3, clustered similarly. Merge same-class candidate groups whose representative pixels are within .02R by transitive union. Representative is the candidate pixel closest to group mean; lexicographic y,x tie. H: one centroid-nearest white pixel per enclosed region of area>=.0005R². Hole topology itself counts all holes. Class priority E,J,H; within-class lexicographic position. Remove radius<.05R, then greedily remove later landmarks at distance<.015R from any retained landmark. These are geometric labels only. No anatomical classes.

Each E/J support is the pruned skeleton of its original connected ink component, excluding central disk. Each H support is its individual hole interior excluding central disk. Support membership, point counts and original component/hole affiliation retained for controls. Distinct source skeleton endpoint/junction degree is NOT conditioned in the pseudo-position model; this is an explicit model limitation, not disguised endpoint generative inference.

## Two frozen endpoints and sparse decision
Radial A: fraction of unordered distinct landmark pairs with |r_i-r_j|<=.03, normalized byR. Angular B: sort bearings, circular gaps g_i; B=mean exp[-.5((g_i−2pi/n)/(.15×2pi/n))²]. This measures equal-gap regularity, not every possible angular relation. Both mathematically invariant to rigid whole-sign rotation. No angle or shell-count scan. Higher is stronger.

Correct90 tests (45 signs ×2 endpoints), including gated signs in denominator. Per endpoint p=(1+number null scores>=observed)/(10000), Bonferroni90p<=.05. Minimum effect observed-minus-null-median>=.15 absolute. PASS also requires90×one-sided95% Clopper–Pearson upper null exceedance probability<=.05. Any fully qualified endpoint permits sparse global PASS; no family search. Otherwise global INCONCLUSIVE if any sign/endpoint inadequate; global FAIL only if every offered endpoint has adequate representation/support and declared effect-detection calibration and none passes.

FAIL safeguard: from locked primary null scores, estimate shift-calibration power for score+0.15 against the corrected .05/90 upper-tail critical value; require>=.80. This is sensitivity under a statistic-location-shift alternative, not general archaeological power. Below .80 means INCONCLUSIVE, never broad absence. No planted configurations or parameter tuning.

Uniform admissible labeled configurations; identical-label swaps induce visible weights. Exact source landmark positions belong to these supports.
