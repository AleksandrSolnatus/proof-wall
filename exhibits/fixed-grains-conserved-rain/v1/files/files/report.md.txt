# Fixed Grains / Conserved Rain 001

2D conservative flow approximation — uncalibrated simulation steps

## Programmed behavior

Stationary overlapping circle unions determine impermeable cells. Rain is finite (area 2), capacity is finite, transfer order is immediate bottom-to-top/left-to-right, gravity has no upward pass, and lateral balancing is prescribed. These rules exclude pressure, capillarity, air trapping, momentum, evaporation, grain motion and physical seconds. N=40 runs use 2,000 ticks; N=80 uses 4,000 ticks, with the same first 160 rain ticks. Dry controls use 160 ticks.

## Independent geometry and predictions

B: adjacent distance .20 < diameter .21; end circles extend .005 beyond walls. A/C: missing row grain gives .19 horizontal opening; elevated grain does not close it.

The exact missing-row-grain opening is 0.19 before rasterization. h=1/N is cell size, not a minimum resolvable gap. Analytic circle geometry and independent four-neighbor flood fill were checked before water. Predicted outcomes were positive drain in A/C, zero drain and positive rejection in B, and distinct A/C routes. No coordinates or parameters were tuned.

## Persistence and limits

See RESTART_REPORT.md for the two fresh-process replay checks. Timing differences cannot establish convergence to physics. Integer exactness is a numerical property, not physical precision. The coarse solid mask narrows openings; overlap is a declared obstacle-union idealization, not settled mechanics. Graph connectivity and water transfer are separate checks; graph paths alone do not prove a route is used. These results do not validate moving sand or real rain infiltration.
