## Water representation and deterministic rules

Each fluid cell capacity K=1,000,000 integer quanta. One quantum represents h*h/K area units per unit out-of-plane thickness. Solid capacity is zero. Initial water and counters are zero. Use integer arithmetic for transfers and the conservation ledger; render conversion to area only for reporting. This deliberately replaces a floating-point 1e-9 ledger tolerance with exact conservation; it is not a claim of physical precision.

One tick consists of the following ordered passes. Source/destination balances update immediately for each transfer. Visit rows bottom-to-top (j=0 is bottom), columns left-to-right; use the same order at both resolutions. Transfer budget per edge per tick: Q=K//4. These are algorithmic rules, not a calibrated time step.

1. Rain: at each of ticks 1..160, supply exactly N*N*K//80 quanta (total area 2). Distribute equally to ALL N top-row cells by integer quotient; assign any remainder in increasing column order. Accept min(offered, free capacity) in each fluid cell; reject all excess and any offer to a solid cell. Increment supplied for every offer. No redistribution of rejected rain.
2. Drain: for eligible bottom cells in increasing column order, remove min(water,Q) and add to cumulative drained.
3. Downward pass: for j=1..N-1, i=0..N-1, if both cells (i,j) and (i,j-1) are fluid, transfer min(source water, destination free capacity,Q) downward.
4. Lateral pass: rows j=0..N-1, edges i=0..N-2. If both neighboring cells are fluid, let d=w_left-w_right. If d>0 transfer min(d//2,Q) left-to-right; if d<0 transfer min((-d)//2,Q) right-to-left. Equal capacity and half-difference ensure source/capacity bounds. No upward transfer and no second downward pass.
5. Record and check invariants after each pass and at tick end. supplied = sum(cell water)+drained+rejected, exactly. Require integer water between 0 and K, zero in solids, monotonically increasing supplied/drained/rejected, and no transfer across solid cells or closed boundaries. No clipping or silent correction.

Freeze these rules in PROTOCOL.md before the first simulation. Upward pressure, capillarity, air trapping, evaporation, momentum, grain displacement and physical seconds are excluded. Lateral balancing and pass order are programmed; unexpected routes, timing, retention and resolution differences are observations under those rules.
