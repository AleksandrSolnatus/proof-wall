THE MAIN RESULTS

                                      Supplied full      New frozen-leaf
  Source                              search states      search states
  16 independent Q gadgets                 113                 7
  Same 16 joined by a selector             114                 7
  8 joined gadgets, final one UNSAT        159                36

The new numbers are LEAF search states, excluding wrapper discovery, source
reading, renaming, model construction and verification. They are not whole-
runtime ratios. Total work still grows with the input. In the first two cases
the wrapper solves Q once. In the last it solves Q and the UNSAT gadget U once
each, then both opposite selector branches use the same checked child proof.

This is a concrete improvement in proof/result reuse. The old complete solver
already solved these fixed-size component families in polynomial total work.
Neither their old behavior nor this improvement settles P versus NP.

VERIFICATION — SEPARATE, OVERLAPPING COHORTS

- Baseline: exact Q reconstruction; full raw-model checks; uncapped recognition
  for 1,2,4,8,16 copies; five positive label shifts; 132 model-consistent partial
  assignments supporting the backdoor argument. Independent graph enumeration
  finds 0 three-colorings of U and 42 of Q. Those are graph-coloring counts,
  not counts of all Boolean models of their CNFs.
- Builder: 758 wrapper executions, 15 full-solver baselines, 686 distinct small
  truth sources / 4,061 assignments; 943 retained nodes, 1,203 checked calls
  and 260 verified reuses. Includes cache-off, signed/sparse, row-reordering,
  nested-separator and LIMITED controls.
- Independent: 1,687 fresh executions, 973 distinct small truth sources /
  6,701 assignments; 30 selected forgeries rejected by both checkers; nine bad
  public inputs rejected. Independently replays all 758 saved DAGs and their
  small truth tables, plus all 15 baselines. Normal/-O outcomes agree.

These totals overlap and must not be added as unique discoveries. The third
interpreter independently checks decomposition, exact reuse maps, model joins,
call order and structural metrics. It uses the supplied check.py for complex
UNSAT leaf certificates; it is not a third implementation of every leaf rule.
