## Implementation corrections

1. **Proof checking.** An older short checker accepted negative parent indices, which Python could interpret as later nodes, permitting a malformed circular proof to pass. The authentic 007B proofs use valid earlier nonnegative references and pass repaired checks. For the reconciled 007B local and shared-DAG formats use `code/verify_proofs_strict.py` in the reconciled supplement: it binds the raw source, validates types and earlier references, and checks exact resolution and conclusions with checks active under `python -O`. Its scope is source-bound entailment; it does not certify scan completeness or every cost counter. Use `code/verify_hall_capacity.py` separately for the capacity witness; the Hall check alone does not establish provenance of appended conflicts.
2. **Residual-unit SCC detection.** The earlier 007B SCC detector could raise `ValueError: path proof lost its resolution pivot` when a residual unit yields a stronger clause before the end of a graph path. Use `code/guard_detector_scc_fixed.py` from the reconciled supplement. It stops lifting once the target or a stronger clause has been proved. The earlier detector failed on the regression; it did not return a false conclusion there. The fixed detector reproduces the original 007B checkpoint's result and counters. This is a restricted shared-pair residual-2SAT recognizer, not a general SAT algorithm.
