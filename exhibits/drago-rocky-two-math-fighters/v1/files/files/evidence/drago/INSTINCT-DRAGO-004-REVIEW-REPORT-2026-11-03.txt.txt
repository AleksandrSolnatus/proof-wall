DISPOSITION

The packet's bounded continuation and work claims are supported by replay and
independent checking. A separate targeted review found a concrete recognition
boundary: a sound learned fact can shrink another loop's clauses below the
detector's exact ternary pattern, so the detector misses a remaining forced fact.
Both rebuild and incremental arms exhibit that limit. No false entailment or
stale-cache defect was found in the inspected implementation and targeted tests.

1. WHAT EPISODE 004 ADDS

This episode connects checked conclusions back into the original shared-candidate
loop recognizer. A globally proved unit simplifies original clauses. That change
can expose a new loop, whose proof refers to the earlier unit. The engine retains
the original source, verified facts, proof prefix, variable-occurrence lists,
current residual clauses, candidate buckets, no-hit cache state, and a versioned
unit/conflict queue. Only clauses incident to the newly fixed variable can change.

An unchanged candidate bucket can keep its no-hit result. A changed bucket is
marked dirty and its local implication graph is rebuilt when checked. This is
selective reconstruction of affected graphs, not retention of a fully dynamic
strongly connected-component decomposition.

The source is immutable within one proof session. Only previously proved GLOBAL
facts may justify reductions. A source digest, exact prior-proof-prefix digest,
and consecutive step number bind each certificate to its context. Clause/path
replay supplies the proof; hashes supply identity and order. Branch decisions,
temporary assumptions and changing-source reuse are outside this contract.

Each accepted nonconflict step fixes a new variable, so there are at most n such
steps and one terminal conflict. Exhausting the rule queue means this ordered
procedure found no further step. It does not establish SAT or all consequences.

2. THE A -> B -> C DEMO IS VALID

For a guard G, the four clauses G OR (+/-X) OR (+/-Y), using every sign pair,
are equivalent to G. If G is false, every X,Y assignment violates one clause;
if G is true, all four clauses hold. The nine-clause demo is therefore equivalent
to A AND (NOT A OR B) AND (NOT B OR C), hence A AND B AND C.

The source contains:
  Four clauses A OR (+/-X) OR (+/-Y).
  Four clauses NOT A OR B OR (+/-X) OR (+/-Y).
  One clause NOT B OR C.

The recognizer first proves A from a ternary loop. Removing the now-false NOT A
from the four wider B clauses creates a new ternary B loop. Proving B then makes
NOT B OR C a unit and proves C. Independent enumeration of all 32 assignments
finds exactly four models: A=B=C=true, and X,Y free.

Saved dependency chain, with zero-based proof IDs:
  Proof 0: A; dependencies [].
  Proof 1: B; dependencies [0].
  Proof 2: C; dependencies [1].

The independent ordinary-resolution conversion contains eleven steps: three for
A, four reductions using the saved A plus three loop resolutions for B, and one
reduction using B for C. Original clauses remain in the proof prefix. Each later
proof references the earlier unit node rather than copying its derivation.

These are operational proof dependencies. A, B and C were all consequences of
the original formula; the first result becomes an explicit checked premise,
not a new independent assumption or a claim of necessity in every possible proof.

Every final deterministic field matches the archived demo exactly: certificates,

The stage boundary is immediately before learned(). Thus A-triggered maintenance
is charged to finding B, B-triggered maintenance to finding C, and C maintenance

7. NEW BOUNDARY: A VALID FACT CAN SHRINK A PROOF OUT OF VIEW

Let block(c,x,y) contain all four c OR (+/-x) OR (+/-y) clauses. Consider:

  F = block(x2,x5,x6) AND block(x3,x2,x4).

The exact raw eight clauses are:
  [2,5,6], [2,5,-6], [2,-5,6], [2,-5,-6],
  [3,2,4], [3,2,-4], [3,-2,4], [3,-2,-4].

Each block is equivalent to its guard, so F is exactly x2 AND x3, with x4,x5,x6
free. Independent enumeration finds eight models, all x2=x3=true.

Both engines first prove x2. The first two clauses of the x3 block are then
satisfied; its remaining clauses reduce to:

  (x3 OR x4), (x3 OR NOT x4).

These directly resolve to x3. But they are two-literal residuals. The current
loop rule admits exactly three distinct residual variables; the unit rule admits
only one literal. Both engines consequently return closure with only x2 fixed.

Renaming x3 to x1 preserves the logical problem under a variable bijection and
the same eight-model count. It changes candidate priority, however. Both engines
then prove x1 before x2 and recognize both facts. Thus this ordered recognizer's
recognized facts can depend on variable names. This is a rule-eligibility and
order limitation, consistent with its declared incompleteness. The implication
remains true, the proof checker stays sound, and the retained cache is accurate.

The missing conclusion has a separately checked six-resolution proof:
  1. Resolve source clauses 0 and 1 on x6 to obtain x2 OR x5.
  2. Resolve sources 2 and 3 on x6 to obtain x2 OR NOT x5.
  3. Resolve those results on x5 to prove x2.
  4. Use that saved x2 to reduce source 6 to x3 OR x4.
  5. Use the same saved x2 to reduce source 7 to x3 OR NOT x4.
  6. Resolve the two binaries on x4 to prove x3.

All eight raw clauses remain in the proof DAG. The ordinary checker and complete
model census pass. BOUNDARY_ARMS.json retains both naming variants, all models
and both complete engine outputs. BOUNDARY_RESOLUTION.json retains the missing
conclusion's source-bound proof.

9. THE NEXT NARROW RULE TO TEST

The boundary suggests extending the guarded loop rule to TWO-literal residuals
as well as three-literal residuals, while retaining ordinary-unit handling.
For a candidate L denied locally within its proof:
  L OR p OR q gives the usual binary implication edges.
  L OR p gives the unit implication NOT p -> p.
  L alone is handled immediately by the ordinary-unit rule.

There is a useful witness-preservation argument. If the denied residual 2-CNF
is unsatisfiable, restricting it by additional checked assignments remains
unsatisfiable. Its surviving clauses still have width at most two, although some
shrink to units or an empty clause. Supporting those smaller residuals preserves
the existence of this kind of witness when clauses simplify, with L unassigned.

This does not preserve the old graph paths or pivot, prove cheap discovery, or
give a complete solver. The engine and checker would both need the explicit
extension, equal acceptance rules in both arms, and new frozen evaluation cases.
The review supplies the argument and counterexample, not an implemented patch
or a measured performance result for the extension.

The packet's proposed activation gate—when to pay for index retention—remains
a separate cost question. I would address this small recognition loss before
tuning that gate. An index policy alone cannot recover a proof pattern excluded
from the rule language.
