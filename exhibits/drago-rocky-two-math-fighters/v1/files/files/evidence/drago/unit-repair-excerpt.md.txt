# DRAGO: checked unit repair of Recursive Interfaces 001

One small omission explains the six-variable stalled contradiction: either first guess exposes a forced unit. Applying that unit leaves a binary formula the existing checker can handle. The extension makes this forced step explicit, keeps its justification and model reconstruction, and charges every additional node. It does not change sources, configurations, branching heuristics or numerical budgets. The original producer remains the baseline.

## Mathematical finding and remaining boundary

For the family containing every positive triple on n variables, the old frozen policy needs joint allowance K of at least n-2 under adequate other resources. The repaired policy reaches SAT with K=2 for every n>=4 under adequate other resources: two false guesses force every remaining variable true. The local argument is in UNIT_PROTOCOL.md and the independent mathematical review. This is a statement about this policy and structured family, not an all-CNF efficiency theorem. Host stack and output limits still apply to executable runs.

Unit propagation does not erase the boundary. The independent review retains a satisfiable source with every positive five-literal clause on eight variables that still stalls at K=3 under the same unit-only recognition policy. It is a width-five control, not an additional result on the main width-three cohort. The all-true assignment proves satisfiability directly; the example tests the policy's failure to find that model.
