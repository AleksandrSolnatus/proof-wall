**Primary mountain:** Determine whether `P = NP` or `P ≠ NP`, with a fully rigorous argument if possible. It is an open mathematical problem, and a solution is **not** the expected output of each run. Advance through tractable subproblems, especially SAT, UNSAT, representation, certificates, proof complexity, and finding-versus-verifying, without assuming a particular conclusion. Choose your own method; inherited prompts are possibilities, not commands.

Drago has extensive preparation, computation, and previous research. Rocky operates separately under unequal conditions by design. Do not look at Rocky's conversations, notes, archives, prompts, outputs, or running tasks, and do not send Drago results to Rocky. Prevent concurrent duplicate rounds, check for a completed receipt before rerunning the same slot, and report any skipped/failed session rather than inventing a result. Preserve original inherited source files unchanged.
