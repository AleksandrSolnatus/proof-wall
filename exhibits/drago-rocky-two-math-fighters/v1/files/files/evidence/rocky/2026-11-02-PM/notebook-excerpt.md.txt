Constructed boundary, established elementary argument / rediscovery: F_k=AND_{i=1..k}(x_i OR y_i). Eliminate x_1,...,x_k before any y. After j eliminations, every subset of {y_1,...,y_j} occurs as a distinct conjunction of residual unit clauses, together with the unchanged pairs for indices >j. Assigning x_i=true removes its pair clause; x_i=false leaves unit y_i. The 2^j subsets are syntactically and semantically distinct: a y in their symmetric difference separates the functions by an appropriate assignment. No same-depth equality merger can combine these distinct Boolean residuals as identical. Every path has weight one. Thus this particular residual-enumeration representation reaches 2^k final states and performs 2^(k+1)-2 restriction calls.

Executed k=1..8 controls exactly matched doubling at every layer. At k=8: 256 final residuals, 510 restriction calls. Weighted counts across remaining assignments gave 3^k original models (6,561 for k=8), as expected from independent pairs. The k=8 control evaluates residual counts; it is not an exhaustive original-16-variable performance benchmark.

Crucial limit: F_k is easy! A satisfying assignment is immediate and the product count is simple. Moreover its Boolean existential projection is TRUE: one residual corresponds to all x true and is itself TRUE. For decision, absorption can discard other OR branches. For exact original counts those other branches still contribute multiplicity, although a different arithmetic factorization can represent it compactly. This is exponential growth of a chosen full residual-state method, not a lower bound against all representations, decision algorithms, counting algorithms, or P=NP. Merely recognizing equality is weaker than factoring, implication, or arithmetic sharing. The family connects to earlier Rocky residual-width work; no originality claim.

Outcome: correctness of weighted cancellation survives on the nontrivial planted sample, while compactness does not follow.
