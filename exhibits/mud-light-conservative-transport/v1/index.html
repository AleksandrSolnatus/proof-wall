Mud changes the light without losing the mud

verified finite result

One uncalibrated72x18 sediment model and a specified110-step default run, not validated fluid or thermal physics.

The question

Can moving sediment change a light field while its modeled mass and direct-beam account remain explicit?

The method

Donor-limited finite-volume-like transfers between suspended/deposited stores, followed by a five-band Beer-Lambert direct-beam calculation.

The finding

The selected007 short-core witness retained nonnegative sediment and exact saved continuation while closing its modeled mass and beam accounts to floating-point residuals.

Research text · rendered from the supplied Markdown

Mud changes the light without losing the mud

Darkening water is easy to draw. A more useful experiment asks where the sediment went and what happened to the light removed from a tracked beam.

AREPO007 holds clay and silt in72 by18 water cells, with separate deposited stores. Settling does not erase material. Erosion returns existing deposited material to the suspension rather than conjuring another source.

Limit the donor, not the balance afterward

Each candidate transfer requests material from one donor. If all requests from that donor exceed its stock, they are scaled together by

alpha = min(1, donor stock / total outgoing requests).

Every accepted transfer debits the donor and credits the receiver by the same amount. This keeps the proposed local moves bounded by availability. It is different from making a negative concentration and clipping it away afterward.

Tiny numerical roundoff is handled separately. Conservation of a declared account is a useful invariant, but it does not prove that the chosen transport law resembles real mud.

What happens to the light?

The five-band direct-beam calculation uses Beer-Lambert attenuation. Across a layer of thickness dz with extinction k,

I_out = I_in exp(-k dz).

The code calculates removal using -expm1(-k dz), then assigns configured portions to water and particle absorption and to scattering out of the resolved beam. Reflection and bed arrival are also accounted for.

That last distinction matters: light scattered out of this beam is not silently labeled heat. Its later path is unknown in this model. A closed direct-beam ledger does not supply a complete radiative-transfer or temperature model.

A small replayable witness

The November10 source-recovery probe extracted the pure numerical script from the original standalone HTML. With source defaults and seed7301, it computed100 steps, saved and loaded a snapshot, then compared ten further steps on both continuations.

For007, the continued serialized states matched exactly and represented sediment remained finite and nonnegative. The final sediment residual was7.105427357601002e-15 and the direct-beam budget residual3.552713678800501e-15 in the model's own units.

This is one110-step default witness, not a long-run or intervention study. The same recovery also inspected descendant models, but their results are not pooled into this exhibit's evidence count. The publication workflow did not rerun the model.

What remains open

The velocities, particle extinction strengths, erosion and absorption/scattering fractions are illustrative. The model is not a resolved three-dimensional fluid, calibrated soil or heat engine. More convincing pictures do not close those missing mechanisms.

The downloadable core is the first script block extracted verbatim from the original HTML, with original comments retained. The UI is omitted and the code is served inert. A selected witness receipt states the inputs, steps, checks and measured residuals without exporting unrelated private recovery records.

Limits & open questions

  • Transport velocities, erosion thresholds and optical partitions are authored; not calibrated sediment hydrodynamics.
  • Scattered-out light leaves the resolved direct beam; its subsequent path and conversion to heat are not modeled.
  • The short witness has no intervention, long-run,3D or general stability guarantee.
  • Source replay checks are not physical validation, and no scientific code was executed in this publication pipeline.

Still open

  • How do transport and optical outcomes change under independently justified parameters and refinement?
