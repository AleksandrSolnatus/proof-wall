# PHAISTOS DIAL — MATH-01 REPORT

**Execution date:** 2026-10-14 (America/Phoenix)  
**Experiment type:** Retrospective computational comparison; a proposed mathematical-expression hypothesis, not a decipherment.  
**Decision: NOT SUPPORTED — for the specified restricted mathematical grammar models.** The broader possibility of unknown mathematical notation remains unresolved.

## 1. Research question and scope

Could the Phaistos Disc contain a mathematical expression, multiple expressions, logical/relational statements, or ordered procedures? We asked whether three *explicitly limited* mathematics-inspired structural grammars predict withheld signs better than frequency, language-like and record-like alternatives. No sign received a number, arithmetic meaning, pictorial meaning, sound, or translation.

**Important distinction:** This experiment tests restricted *structural surrogates* of mathematical grammar; it does not test all possible mathematical writing systems. The winning language-like model does not establish that the Disc is language.

## 2. Authorized evidence and integrity

- Input: immutable `inputs/corpus_masked.json`, raw SHA-256 `18ee5ac70b21696e7ed60616e3a265eaf0261fdd49779464e254a7e916f74a24` (verified before analysis).
- 60 eligible cells, 237 sign occurrences: Pen 118, Pencil 119; all S01–S45 occur.
- Pen.C08 excluded completely; **no** Pen.C07|C09 artificial boundary created. 57 valid face-internal consecutive cell boundaries remain (28 Pen, 29 Pencil).
- Pencil.C24 restored to S07 S45 S07 using the previously accepted blind reveal, explicitly documented as an analytic view over a still-unchanged input file.
- Pencil.C28 final S07 and final S25 evaluated separately with fixed settings. None was chosen according to outcome.
- Archival within-cell order was used as written. This is **not** a determination of ancient reading direction; no faces were joined into one sequence.
- The pre-existing corpus source had already been examined in prior Dial work. Our 5-fold held-out computation is **not an independent, truly prospective blind test**.

## 3. Models and predictive evaluation

Each of 45 signs is treated as a categorical outcome. After excluding an entire dependency family from model fitting, the model is scored on negative log2 probability of each held-out sign **conditional on its already-visible context** (head/previous sign if required). Scores are in **bits per sign, smaller is better**, averaged over 237 eligible occurrences.

All models share training-only global sign probabilities with Jeffreys 0.5 pseudocounts, and specialized predictive distributions are shrunk toward those frequencies with a fixed 20-equivalent-observation prior. This regularizes costly conditional models without tuning hyperparameters to the result. Exact-copy/substring/edit-distance≤1 whole-cell dependencies are connected into a conservative graph (union under the two C28 variants); the known multi-cell Pen C14–C16 / C20–C22 duplicate is linked. **38 dependency families**, largest 12 cells, were distributed across 5 folds of 48/47/47/47/48 signs.

Mathematics-inspired models: **A** alternate value/operator slots anchored to the first archived slot; **B** a prefix procedural instruction at the head with later arguments conditioned on the head; **C** a central relation sign with other signs in operand-like slots. Alternatives: **D** language-like within-cell bigram transitions; **E** nonmathematical first/middle/last record fields; **F** sign-frequency-only null. **W** adds a cross-cell bigram distribution to D for actual consecutive physical cells of the same face; no across-face edge is supplied. These are hand-specified restricted hypotheses, not historically authenticated Phaistos grammars.

## 6. Phase 3: one face, many statements, both faces, procedure

The tested W model asks whether a sign at the next physically consecutive cell start becomes easier to predict using the prior cell's last sign than using an ordinary cell-start distribution. Here it does **not**. This is a limited negative predictive test for a particular notion of cross-cell continuity; it cannot choose between **one statement per face** and **multiple statements per face**. B tests a constrained procedural-head syntax, which also does not outperform D. No validated between-face reading connection exists in the evidence, so a whole-two-face mathematical expression is **not testable by this corpus alone** without an added unsupported premise.

## 7. Phase 4: blind challenge and what was actually withheld

Five dependency-group folds kept complete related cells out of model fitting. We then predicted each held-out sign's identity **given the other contextual signs available in that held-out cell**. This is a legitimate held-out conditional-scoring exercise, but it is not a prospective independent blind challenge: the corpus was previously exposed, grammar families were conceived with project knowledge, and no new undisclosed material was introduced. Therefore **no true blind decipherment or mathematical-content claim** can follow.

## 8. Phase 5: inference levels and decision

1. **Structural regularities**: yes (e.g., repeated contexts and sign-position associations).
2. **Specifically mathematical grammar**: **not supported** by these A/B/C predictive comparisons; the best alternative D predicts better in both C28 versions and all five folds.
3. **Mathematical content**: not established, and indeed not addressed semantically by any sign-to-value mapping.

**Decision: NOT SUPPORTED (the specified mathematical grammar family)**. This is *not* proof that mathematics is absent. Unknown grammars, undocumented direction, tiny dependent corpus, lack of external calibration, and missing genuine prospective blind data limit the inference. A broader claim that the Disc communicates mathematics remains **unresolved**.
