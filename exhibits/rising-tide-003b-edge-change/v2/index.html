Rising Tide 003B: what survives a graph edge change

Original question

Which retained graph-power calculations remain useful after an edge changes, once maintenance and checking are charged?

Method and recorded result

The recorded experiment compares rebuilding, fresh binary computation and exact repair using integer support counts on fixed graph-edit workloads. Its source, protocol, final ledgers and preserved pilot remain together.

Retained Boolean-power structure can be repaired across edits; savings depend on propagation and subsequent use. Maintaining unused powers can lose badly.
