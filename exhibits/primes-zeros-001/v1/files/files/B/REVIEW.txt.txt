Interpretation review
The finite expression concerns Chebyshev psi, not pi. At half-integers there
are no prime-power jumps, so no midpoint ambiguity enters these sampled values.
Pairing rho=0.5+it with its conjugate gives twice the real part:
2*sqrt(x)*(0.5*cos(t*log(x))+t*sin(t*log(x)))/(0.25+t*t).
The supplied implementation subtracts that pair contribution and includes
-log(2*pi)-0.5*log(1-x^(-2)); its sign conventions agree with INPUT.
MAE and maximum error decline across these four sampled truncations, but
81,79,81 points worsen compared with the previous truncation. Thus adding
these terms is not pointwise monotone improvement, and this small grid does
not establish a general convergence rate or certified approximation bound.
