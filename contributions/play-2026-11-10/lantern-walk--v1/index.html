What exactly is staying the same?

Both complete graphs have vertices and the same number of edges. Every vertex has degree 2, and neither graph has triangles. These summaries stay equal even after the lantern reveals the difference.

The local view is the induced radius-r ball around the gold vertex, with vertex names forgotten. Until the smaller ring closes, both local views are the same rooted path. This is exact equality for these fixtures, not a general graph-equivalence test.

For a small ring of length m, the first distinguishing radius is floor(m/2). The larger ring has length 2m. The proof and the replay are in the note and the Python experiment.
